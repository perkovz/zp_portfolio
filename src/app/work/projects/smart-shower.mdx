The main challenge was to create an advanced shower GUI on an NXP board and 5” screen running on a Linux Yocto system. This task needed to be completed quickly and effectively. Tom and I tackled this problem head-on and figured it out in just five weeks!

## Designing for Embedded Hardware

Finding the right design tool for prototyping on hardware was quite the adventure!

- I started by connecting the board to my laptop using HDMI and USB cables to mirror my Figma prototypes.
- Next, I dove into researching HMI design tools and landed on Crank. However, learning how to use Crank Storyboard wasn’t straightforward, especially since it’s not a free tool. I reached out to their sales team and successfully obtained a free license!
- The main reason I’ve choose Crank Storyboard were its embedded features, which, with a little bit of Lua scripting enabled me to create a prototype.

## Design solution

I developed a comprehensive GUI prototype that included:

- **Intuitive Navigation**: Designed screens in Figma, focusing on easy access to Home, Presets, and other essential features.
- **Interactive Features**: Leveraged Crank to implement key flows and wrote Lua scripts to enhance user interaction.
- **Seamless Integration**: Collaborated with Tom to set up the NXP MCIMX8ULP - SOM board, using a 5” NXP BV screen running on a Linux Yocto system.

## Impact and Results

The new prototype effectively resolved business AND user pain points:

- We made the prototype in **5 weeks**, in-house, with a budget of **$0**.
- We substantially shortened development times by introducing new tool and new ways of working to the company, where designers can generate code ready designs.
- We were able to test designs easily and quickly retiring risks early and further lowering development costs.
