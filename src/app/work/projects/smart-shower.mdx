# Overview

Moen’s smart shower development program needed a solution that would enable product and design teams to quickly validate, test, and iterate shower features, GUI, and advanced interactions. Without specific expertise on the team, the project was stuck in limbo, so we decided to take the initiative and innovate within the design team. The solution, based on Crank, shortened firmware GUI development time by 30% in just five weeks.

![NXP MCIMX8ULP - SOM board, and 5” NXP BV screen.](/images/projects/shower-gui/nxp.jpeg)

---

## My Contribution

As a lead on this initiative:
- I researched the most known tools including Protopie, QT Design Studio and Embedded Wizard. Once set on Crank, I reached out to their sales team and successfully obtained a free license.
- I collaborated with one firmware engineer on the NXP board setup.
- I learned the basics of Crank Storyboard and Lua scripting and created a working prototype of the smart shower interface.
- Once my protoype was functional, I ran early-stage user tests.
- And finally, I documented and evangelized solutions within the company.

This innovation also enabled designers to deliver Figma components directly to engineers, improving collaboration and the quality of the final product.

## Key Features

- **Starting a Shower**: Start a shower from the home screen.
- **Choosing a Preset**: Users can customize their shower experience by saving preferred settings for temperature, water flow, and duration, allowing for a tailored and enjoyable shower each time.
- **Integrations**: The shower system seamlessly integrates with smart home devices, enabling voice control and automation for a convenient and hands-free experience.
- **Precise Water Temperature Control**: Users can achieve their ideal shower temperature with precise control, ensuring comfort and satisfaction while conserving water.
- **UI Interactions**: Test Cranks abilities to create complex UI interactions.

## Designing for Embedded Hardware

Learning to design in Crank Storyboard and creating a prototype on hardware was quite an adventure! 

![Component list prepared for Crank Import.](/images/projects/shower-gui/prepping-for-crank.png)

- I started by connecting the board to my laptop using HDMI and USB cables to mirror my Figma prototypes.
- Next, Tom (firmware engineer) connected Crank with Yocto OS, enabling me to work independently and stage the whole Crank application through FileZilla to the board as needed for testing.
- I’ve used embedded functionality within the Stroyboard to create navigation and transitions.
- The significant breakthrough was adding custom transitions and events with Lua scripting, which was the final functionality needed for a working prototype.

## Design solution

Once in Crank, I developed a comprehensive GUI prototype that included:

- **Intuitive Navigation**: Focusing on easy access to Home, Presets, and other essential features.
- **Interactive Features**: Including screen transitions, toggle animation and temperature sliders.
- **Seamless Integration**: With third-party system such as Spotify music.

## Impact and Results

The new prototype effectively resolved business AND user pain points:

- We made the prototype in **5 weeks**, in-house, with a budget of **$0**.
- We substantially shortened development times by introducing new tool and new ways of working to the company, where designers can generate code ready designs.
- We were able to test designs easily and quickly retiring risks early and further lowering development costs.
