## Overview

The Moen Smart Faucet with Motion Control is an advanced kitchen faucet that offers touchless operation through innovative sensor technology. Users can control water flow and temperature using simple hand motions, voice commands via smart speakers, or through a smartphone app.

The faucet integrates with Amazon and Google devices for additional control options and customizable presets, and it’s an integral part of Moen’s Smart Water Network app.

## My Role: End-to-end product design

I was part of an ambitious project to redesign motion gesture experience and educate users to get the most out of their Smart Faucet. As a Product Designer, my role involved:

- Developing intuitive user flows, illustrations and animations for the handle-less temperature and flow control.
- Creating clear, user-friendly instructions, tips and tutorials within the SWN application.
- Conducting extensive usability testing and iterating designs based on user feedback.
- Optimizing the app's design for integration with Amazon Alexa and Google Home.
- Collaborating with engineering, QA, project and product management

### Sensor Accuracy

Our preliminary research discovered two critical challenges users encountered when interacting with the handle free faucet:

- **Mental Model**: Because it is first and one of the kind, faucet without handle requires user training, challenging existing mental models of faucet operation.
- **Success Rate**: Expected success rate for the motion-controlled faucet and exceed actual performance were not the same, highlighting additional need to train the users on motion gestures.

## Solution and Planning

Because of those obstacles we decided the best way to train the users to change their mental models and also improve motion gesture success rate is through multiple re-training process throught the app.

I divided design work into 3 stages:

- Improve Temperature, Sensor and Flow control app experience.
- Update Illustration and Motion Graphics library. Create first Motion design system. Write new clear and concise content with French translations.
- Connect everything together and thoroughly test high-fidelity prototypes through UserTesting and Guerrilla tests. Make sure both light and dark mode pass accessibility tests.

As a prep for this project, I studied apps of five smart home brands and products including thermostats, doorbells, cameras, alarm systems, motion tracking webcams and floodlights. Challenge here was to get products with motion tracking capabilities I could test, so I visited local Target and BestBuy to play... I mean test, their products.
