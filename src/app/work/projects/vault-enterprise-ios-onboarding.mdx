**Timeline:** 1 week <br />
**Platform:** iOS

# Overview

Users reported difficulty in locating key features and were unaware that pulling the screen down could refresh active locks.

In response to those usability issues, I collaborated with Brandon (iOS dev) and designed a new onboarding tutorial for the Master Lock Enterprise iOS app.

## My Contribution

As a design lead on this short collaboration I influenced art direction within the Master Lock branding rules, used Figma to create new layouts, images and styles, and UserTesting platform for validation.

---

## Objectives

1. **Enhance User Understanding**: Create a resource that helps users quickly grasp the app’s features.
2. **Reduce Support Queries**: Minimize the volume of customer support requests related to navigation and usage.
3. **Improve User Experience**: Foster a more intuitive interaction with the app, leading to higher satisfaction and retention rates.

![Requirements polished in Figma.](/images/projects/masterlock-tutorial/requirements-and-links.jpg)

## Design Process

![Sketching on the paper.](/images/projects/masterlock-tutorial/research-and-sketching.png)

Key stages of the process included:

1. **Research and Analysis**: We analyzed customer feedback and identified common pain points, which helped shape our approach.
2. **Wireframing**: Developed wireframes to outline the tutorial’s structure, ensuring a logical flow of information.
3. **Content Creation**: Drafted clear instructions and visual aids, making sure to use simple language that resonates with users.
4. **Testing and Iteration**: I conducted accessibility and guerilla testing with real users to gather insights and refine the tutorial.

![Many, many iterations.](/images/projects/masterlock-tutorial/iterations.png)

## Key Features

• **Step-by-Step Guidance**: Each section provides straightforward instructions accompanied by visuals, making it easy for users to follow along. <br />
• **Highlighting Important Functions**: We emphasize crucial features, such as refreshing locks and navigating menus, to boost user confidence. <br />
• **Interactive Elements**: Incorporating interactive components to engage users and enhance their learning experience.

## Outcome

Since the tutorial’s implementation, we have observed a **significant decrease (0!) in support inquiries related to onboarding**. Users have reported feeling more empowered when using the app, indicating that the tutorial has successfully met its objectives. Feedback has highlighted the clarity of instructions and the effectiveness of visual aids as key strengths.
