# The Problem

Customers reported difficulty in locating key features within the app and were unaware of refreshing the active locks list when the connection is disrupted.

In response to those usability issues, I collaborated with Brandon (iOS dev) and, on top of the regular sprint tasks, designed a new onboarding tutorial for the Master Lock Enterprise iOS app.

![Lock in the awake state with the mobile app screen.](/images/projects/masterlock-tutorial/problem.png)

## My Contribution

As a design lead on this short collaboration:

- Art direction within the Master Lock branding rules.
- Created new user flow, layouts, images and styles.
- Verified new tutorial through UserTesting and in-person.

---

## Objectives

1. **Enhance User Understanding**: Create a resource that helps users quickly grasp the app’s features.
2. **Reduce Support Queries**: Minimize the volume of customer support requests related to navigation and usage.

---

## Design Process

Key stages of the process included:

1. **Research and Analysis**: We analyzed customer feedback and identified common pain points, which helped shape our approach.
2. **Wireframing**: Developed wireframes to outline the tutorial’s structure, ensuring a logical flow of information.
3. **Content Creation**: Drafted clear instructions and visual aids, making sure to use simple language that resonates with users.
4. **Testing and Iteration**: I conducted accessibility and guerilla testing with real users to gather insights and refine the tutorial.

![Many, many iterations.](/images/projects/masterlock-tutorial/iterations.png)

---

## Key Features

![Snippets of key features featured in the tutorial.](/images/projects/masterlock-tutorial/zoom.jpg)

• **Step-by-Step Guidance**: Each section provides straightforward instructions accompanied by visuals, making it easy for users to follow along. <br />
• **Highlighting Important Functions**: We emphasize crucial features, such as refreshing locks and navigating menus, to boost user confidence. <br />
• **Interactive Elements**: Incorporating interactive components to engage users and enhance their learning experience.

## Outcome

In the months after the launch we reached out to 4 control users who reported the issues. They have reported feeling more empowered when using the app, indicating that the tutorial has successfully met its objectives. Feedback has highlighted the clarity of instructions and the effectiveness of visual aids as key strengths.

- In the 3 month period after the update, we received **0 support inquiries related to feature issues**.
